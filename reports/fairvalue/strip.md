# Monte Carlo fair-value strip (GPU1, GPU2)

**MODEL OUTPUT ON A PROXY: not the settlement index, not a forecast to trade on.** As of 2026-10-11. Method: docs/montecarlo_fair_value.md (D55).
20000 antithetic paths per scenario; Kemna-Vorst geometric control variate. Values are relative to today's proxy spot S0 (F/S0), so they can be applied to the settlement index's level once known. Real-world expectations: a futures price different from these is a risk premium or information, not automatically a mispricing.
